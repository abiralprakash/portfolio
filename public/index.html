LegalTech SaaS · Flagship

Nepal IP Management System

  1. ProblemIP firms tracked renewals in spreadsheets, files across drives, and DoI status in memory — until a client called.
  2. My approachModel the desk: company → file → matter → asset. Build deadline engines and register search for live client calls.
  3. System builtFull practice platform on Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2 — 300+ APIs, 150+ migrations, OCR intake wired in.
  4. Real impact130K+ trademark records managed; used daily by IP firms including Global Law Associates.

Behind the build

How a legal workflow became a software platform

  • A problem appeared. Renewals lived in memory; files were scattered.
  • A system was designed. Domain model mirrored the physical desk.
  • A tradeoff was made. Specialized LegalTech over horizontal CRM.
  • A product shipped. Live desks on nepalipms.com.
Product Case study nepalipms.com

Applied AI · Document intelligence

AI OCR Document Intelligence Engine

  1. ProblemGovernment trademark bulletins arrived as dense bilingual PDFs; staff re-typed rows by hand during filing season.
  2. My approachSeparate extraction from intelligence. OCR maps fields; humans confirm before anything merges into a matter.
  3. System builtUpload → R2 → layout OCR → classification → AI extraction → review queue → register search & reminders.
  4. Real impactProduction intake inside NepalIPMS — AI acceleration with expert control intact.

Behind the build

Why OCR is not enough for document intelligence

  • A problem appeared. Text extraction alone still left desks re-typing structure.
  • A system was designed. Staged pipeline with confidence and review.
  • A tradeoff was made. More components for safer merges.
  • A product shipped. Prototype → production inside NepalIPMS.

Legal platform · Publishing

Global Law Platform

  1. ProblemA practicing IP firm needed a credible digital presence and a place to publish legal knowledge — not a brochure site.
  2. My approachContent architecture first: practice areas, knowledge center, SEO-ready pages the firm can maintain.
  3. System builtCorporate site + CMS on Cloudflare — 100+ legal pages with structured publishing workflows.
  4. Real impactLive at globallaw.com.np; firm also runs daily IP operations on NepalIPMS.

Behind the build

Building a knowledge platform, not just a website

  • A problem appeared. Brochure sites don’t help a firm publish or get found.
  • A system was designed. CMS + knowledge architecture first.
  • A tradeoff was made. Depth of content over decorative marketing.
  • A product shipped. Live publishing surface at globallaw.com.np.
