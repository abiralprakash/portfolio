How I work.

Principles that show up in products, decisions, and writing — so visitors understand the method, not only the artifacts.

Product principles

Desks before demos.

  • Understand workflow before technologySit with the real files, failure modes, and who decides “done.”
  • Build for real usersName the person who opens the screen tomorrow — or stop designing.
  • Measure usefulnessDaily completion and trust beat launch applause.

Engineering principles

Constraints are the design.

  • Simple systems scale betterFewer moving parts a small team can operate at 2am.
  • Production constraints matterLatency, migrations, and bilingual PDFs are product inputs.
  • Tradeoffs are part of designDocument them — see decisions.

AI principles

Improve decisions — stay accountable.

  • AI should improve decisionsRemove a hated step; do not add a chat box for its own sake.
  • Human review mattersNothing silent into systems of record — pattern.
  • Workflow integration beats demosShip inside the tool operators already open — OCR in NepalIPMS.
