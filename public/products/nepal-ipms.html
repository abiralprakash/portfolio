What is it?

One record for the desk.

NepalIPMS is software for intellectual property practice operations in Nepal. It holds client companies, files, matters, and assets — with statutory deadlines, national trademark register search, document storage, and OCR-assisted bulletin intake.

Live at nepalipms.com.

Who is it for?

IP firm operators.

Associates, paralegals, and partners who manage trademarks and related matters daily — not casual website visitors. Organizations using it for operations include Global Law Associates and Janak Bhandari & Associates (per product register).

Problem

Spreadsheets are not a practice system.

Renewals lived in memory. Files were split across drives and inboxes. Department of Industry status was often discovered when a client called. The product replaces that fragmentation with a desk-shaped model.
