Photography brief

Magazine profile, not LinkedIn.

Highest-ROI visual upgrade for this portfolio. Share this page with a photographer in Kathmandu.

Avoid

  • Passport cropStiff, centered, white background
  • Corporate poseFolded arms, fake laugh, office blur
  • Heavy retouchPlastic skin, neon grade

Aim for

  • Natural lightWindow soft light; neutral wall or quiet interior
  • Minimal paletteDeep blue / ink / soft gray — matches site accent #0A3A5C
  • Editorial distanceThink profile feature, not staff directory

Shot list

Four frames that earn their place.

  1. Looking away — contemplative three-quarter; used as About hero atmosphere
  2. Direct portrait — calm eye contact; primary About / Open Graph
  3. At the desk — working, not posing; laptop optional, notebook preferred
  4. Reviewing sketches — notebook, diagram, or paper — pairs with Artifacts
