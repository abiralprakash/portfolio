Design system

How this brand presents itself.

Not a component marketplace — the approach behind the portfolio and product surfaces.

Typography

Newsreader + Inter.

  • Why Newsreader italicEditorial readability for headings — human voice over startup chrome.
  • Why Inter for bodyTechnical credibility and long-form clarity at product scale.
  • AccentDeep blue #0A3A5C — calm authority, not purple glow.

Layout

Narrative over dashboard.

  • One job per sectionHeadline, short support, one visual or list — not card grids.
  • Alternating work rowsImage-and-copy rhythm for Selected Work — not tiles.
  • Reveal motionPresence and hierarchy, not noise.

Components

Surfaces that recur.

  • Timeline / chainBuild log and changelog — when, what, why, impact.
  • Case study blocksChallenge → solution → architecture → impact.
  • Architecture diagramsInteractive stages preferred over static equal boxes.
  • Product rowsOverview, role, technologies, outcomes, links.
  • Related readingKnowledge-graph navigation — not “latest posts.”

Principles

What we refuse to fake.

  • Clarity before decorationIf removing a border or badge does not hurt understanding, remove it.
  • Evidence before claimsNo vanity users, no placeholder testimonials, no invented metrics.
  • Systems before featuresShip the workflow shape; resist feature salad.
