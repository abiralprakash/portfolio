LegalTech SaaS

Nepal IP Management System

A firm opens one portal for trademarks, patents, deadlines, litigation, and national register search — replacing spreadsheets and scattered drives with matter records that mirror how IP desks already organize client portfolios.

Before

Renewal dates in personal calendars. Register lookups in shared PDFs. Bulletin entries re-typed by hand. No single view of a client’s portfolio during a live call.

After

130,000+ trademark rows searchable in milliseconds. Matter-linked deadlines with notifications. OCR-assisted bulletin intake. One system used daily by IP firms in Nepal.

1
Open a client portfolio

Navigate company → file → matter hierarchy. Every asset — trademark, patent, design — lives in context with its renewal clock and filing history.

2
Search the national register

Run a live mark or application number query against 130k+ records. Results return fast enough for phone conversations with clients.

3
Review upcoming deadlines

Renewal windows surface per matter. Staff see what needs action this week without digging through inboxes.

4
Track Department of Industry status

Application status updates link back to the matter record — so practitioners know where a filing stands before the client asks.

5
Attach documents and notes

Filings, certificates, and correspondence stay with the matter in R2-backed storage — not lost across personal drives.

Document intelligence

AI OCR Document Intelligence Engine

Government trademark bulletins arrive as dense PDFs. The pipeline extracts structured fields, routes low-confidence rows to human review, and merges confirmed data into live matter records inside NepalIPMS.

Before

Staff manually read bulletin pages, copy application numbers and classes into spreadsheets, and hope nothing was misread during a busy filing season.

After

Upload a bulletin PDF, OCR extracts bilingual text blocks, a worker maps fields to register schema, practitioners confirm in a review queue, and records merge automatically.

1
Upload a Department of Industry bulletin

PDF pages land in R2 with a processing job attached. The system knows which bulletin issue and date range is being ingested.

2
OCR extracts layout-aware text

Azure OCR reads Nepali and English blocks, preserving table structure where bulletins use dense tabular layouts.

3
Parse worker maps fields

Application numbers, classes, applicant names, and status lines map to the register schema with confidence scores per field.

4
Human review queue

Low-confidence extractions surface for practitioner confirmation. Experts correct errors before anything touches production matters.

5
Merge into search and reminders

Confirmed rows update the register index and link to existing matters — feeding search and renewal notifications downstream.
