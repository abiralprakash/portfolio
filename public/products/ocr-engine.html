What is it?

Intelligence after OCR.

Not a standalone OCR app. A staged pipeline: store pages, extract layout text, classify sections, map fields with confidence, require human review, then feed register search and deadline engines inside NepalIPMS.

Who is it for?

Operators who cannot trust silent merges.

IP practice staff who ingest Department of Industry bulletins. They need speed without abdicating judgment — every field can show provenance before it enters a matter.
