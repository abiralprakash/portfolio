How Linear designs workflows

Opinionated issue flow — speed as a product principle, not only a performance metric.

Product

Linear — issue tracking for product engineering teams that refuse Jira-shaped friction.

Problem solved

Issue tools often encode process theater. Teams want capture → triage → ship with minimal ceremony.

UX decisions

  • Keyboard-firstPower operators never leave flow; latency is felt as disrespect.
  • Narrow status modelFewer states than enterprise trackers — opinions as product.
  • Cycles over waterfall fieldsTimeboxing matches how modern teams actually plan.

Technical observations

Client responsiveness and optimistic UI are part of the brand. Real-time sync makes the board feel like a shared instrument, not a form database.

Lessons for builders

For domain SaaS (IP desks, education ops), borrow Linear’s courage to constrain workflow — then map those constraints onto real statutory or academic calendars. See Workflow Automation Pattern · NepalIPMS.
