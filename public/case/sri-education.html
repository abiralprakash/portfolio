Overview

Clarity for high-stakes student decisions.

SRI Educational Consulting needed a platform that communicates outcomes — admissions, destinations, visa success — while guiding students through counselling, applications, and test preparation with honesty and structure.

Challenge

Trust is the product.

Study-abroad decisions are expensive and emotional. Families need proof of outcomes and a clear process — not vague promises. The site had to balance aspiration with concrete services: admissions counselling, visa support, accommodation, insurance, and test prep.

Solution

A roadmap-shaped consultancy platform.

Sri Education guides students through counselling, profile evaluation, applications, visa support, and pre-departure — with outcome proof, destination guides, and consultation flows that hand qualified inquiries to counsellors.

The production site at srieducation.com.np balances aspiration with concrete services: admissions, visa, accommodation, insurance, and test prep.

User Story

From vague promises to a visible roadmap.

Before

Families evaluating study abroad saw undifferentiated service lists and aspiration-heavy copy with no clear process. Consultation requests arrived without context — counsellors spent first calls explaining basic steps instead of advising on fit.

After

A roadmap-shaped platform from counselling through pre-departure — outcome proof and visa-success stories where trust is earned, destination guides with pathway framing, and consultation intake that captures where a student is in the journey.

Decision Log

Process beats promises.

Roadmap-shaped IA over flat service list

Decision: Structure the site as a student journey — counselling → applications → visa → pre-departure — with services as modules along the path.

Reason: Families need to see where they are and what comes next; undifferentiated lists feel like every other consultancy.

Tradeoff: Less flexible “everything on one page” marketing; clearer self-qualification before consultation.

Outcome proof early in the journey

Decision: Place visa-success metrics and stories where families evaluate credibility — not buried below the fold.

Reason: Study-abroad decisions are expensive and emotional; proof must appear before aspiration alone carries the page.

Tradeoff: Less minimalist hero design; higher trust at the decision moment.

Consultation intake tied to journey stage

Decision: Capture qualified inquiry context — destination, level, timeline — at the CTA instead of a generic contact form.

Reason: CTAs convert better when visitors already understand which step they are on; counsellors skip repetitive discovery calls.

Tradeoff: Slightly more form fields; materially better follow-up quality.

Failed Attempts

Approaches families rejected.

Aspiration-only homepage without visible process. An early version led with dream imagery and vague “world-class opportunities” copy. Bounce rates stayed high — families wanted to see steps and proof before booking consultation.

Single long-scroll page for every destination. We tried consolidating USA, UK, Australia, and Canada content into one page. Mobile drop-off spiked; destination-specific guides with focused CTAs performed better for both SEO and counsellor handoff.

Architecture

Roadmap-shaped information architecture.

  • ProofOutcome metrics and visa-success stories placed where trust is earned early.
  • ServicesEnd-to-end offerings from counselling through pre-departure, structured as clear modules.
  • DestinationsCountry guides for USA, UK, Australia, Canada, and more — pathway framing, not tourism copy.
  • IntakeConsultation and contact flows that hand qualified inquiries to counsellors.

Lessons learned

Process beats promises.

Families choosing study abroad need to see a clear process and honest proof — not vague aspiration. Structuring the site as a student roadmap, with services as modules along that path, reduced the undifferentiated service-list problem common in consultancy sites.

Consultation CTAs work best when visitors already understand what step they are on.
